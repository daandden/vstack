# Read the Flow page

Read this reference when navigating the page or explaining its keyboard controls.

A single 44 px bar stays visible as you scroll. It names the project, links the Flow title to Overview, and names the Focused Stage. The Stage name uses its recorded ticket title when available and its Stage label otherwise. Hover a truncated name for its full text. Assistive technology reads the full name.

The bar shows chips for the Focused Stage's open questions. It marks the question at the reading stop and shows `!` for a recorded concern, including on a re-ask after a concern. Up to three other waiting Stages show their open-question counts. Extra question chips fold into a count that opens Jump. Extra waiting Stages fold into a count that opens the Map. The theme control follows the system preference until you pick Light or Dark, and the page remembers that choice.

A link with no fragment opens the tab of the first open question in Flow-wide order, or Overview when no question is open. A fragment opens the tab that holds its target.

A Stage starts with its state, parent, and children. Its reading order is the order in which it first published each record. Each round has one header with its number, publication time and age when recorded, and Q numbers in the order they were asked. Questions do not print their publication positions. Use `show` to read the recorded positions.

Open questions show their full content. Settled questions and earlier attempts fold in place. The folded row keeps its own outcome, such as an explicit pick, an agent reading, a concern, or a timeout. An earlier attempt links to its latest attempt. Adjacent Assumed picks fold together. To reopen an Assumed pick, name it in a picker note. Expand a row or follow a link to see the full record where it was published.

One notice identifies historical content in retained order when exact publication order was not recorded. New content follows it in recorded order. Answers and Result revisions update their records in place. Earlier Result revisions remain available in disclosures. Overview holds progress, read-only observations, and the Stage list. Topics lists every Topic note.

Corrections also mark Backgrounds, Topic notes, question context, answer readings, Assumed picks, and close decisions. Follow **Correction** to the published replacement and its reciprocal link to return. A corrected answer reading is current in Overview and the question's answer area. **Original agent reading, no longer current** retains the earlier interpretation beside the unchanged user response. Affected historical summaries keep their recorded text and provenance, mark that a Correction affects them, and show the current answer separately. A withdrawal grants no permission. A new decision still needs an explicit re-ask.

Overview also has **Changed since last visit**, a browser-local index of new and revised blocks. Its links open the existing blocks in their original positions. Revised historical blocks stay labeled as historical and never receive invented publication positions. The first visit establishes a baseline without labeling old content as new. Each loaded authored revision becomes the baseline for the next visit; the index keeps comparing with the previous visit while the page stays open. Reloading starts the next visit. Clearing browser storage resets the baseline. Viewing and remembering a revision change no shared Flow content, answers, or consent.

Revised Results also have **Compare revisions**. Choose an earlier snapshot to compare with the complete current Result. The comparison names both revisions and focused edits. It lists changed fields, sections, rows, or measurements, with removed text struck through and added text underlined. Unchanged content is omitted from the comparison, not from the Result. **Complete earlier snapshot** opens the captured revision, and **Complete current Result** returns to its stable link. Citations, attachments, and Reader comments remain with their full snapshots. A revised Result in **Changed since last visit** opens the comparison against the snapshot remembered by this browser.

The Focused Stage is the Stage the page shows. In a Stage, `n` cycles only that Stage's open questions and wraps to its first. When the Stage has no open questions, `n` stays put and the page says so. A Stage link, a fragment, or `h` and `l` changes the Focused Stage.

When a content update publishes a new open question in the Focused Stage, the page selects it and scrolls to it without a key press, even if you are reading another block. Questions in other Stages and questions becoming answered, retired, or superseded do not move the page. While focus is in an input, textarea, select, or editable content, the page holds navigation, including fragment changes from outside the page. When focus leaves editable content, the page applies only the latest held move.

On Overview and Topics, `n` uses the Flow-wide order: newest in-progress Stage first, then older in-progress Stages. Once it opens a question's Stage tab, the next `n` stays in that Stage. Finished Stages, retired questions, settled answers, and superseded asks are excluded in both cases. Stage labels include ticket ids when known. Unticketed repeats get distinct labels.

Choose **Stages** or press `m` to open the Map panel at its Stages tab. Stages waiting for an answer come first, followed by unfinished Stages with no open questions. Finished Stages fold by ticket, with a separate group for ticketless work. Each Stage shows its state and open-question count. Open Topics from the Stages tab when the Flow has Topic notes.

Inside the Map panel, `h` and `l` switch tabs, `j` and `k` select items, and Enter follows a link or expands a finished group. The Matt flow path tab shows the path and current position when the Flow has a path. A path step with repeated Stages links to each one. Arrow keys pan a graph larger than the panel. Escape, Close, a click outside the panel, or `m` closes it. The `t` key does nothing.

A Flow with structured wayfinder data also has a Wayfinder map tab. It draws named ticket links, blocker-to-ticket arrows, and each ticket's map state. `j` and `k` select tickets in publication order. Enter opens the selected ticket's tracker link in a new tab. The graph keeps its label size inside the scrollable panel. Arrow keys pan it. An empty published graph says that no tickets are charted yet. The Map panel never derives blockers from the Stage tree or from a captured figure.

Choose **Jump** or press `g` to open the reading index. In a Stage, the Jump row follows its reading order and lists Background, Results, one Assumed picks link, and each question's latest attempt with its state. Earlier attempts remain at their original fragments, including when a later Stage re-asks a question. The index marks its current entry when one matches the reading stop. Choose an entry to open its exact fragment. Escape, Close, a click outside the panel, or `g` closes the index.

With both panels closed, `j` and `k`, or the down and up arrows, move between reading stops. The page updates the URL fragment and moves focus to the target without drawing a focus ring. `h` and `l`, or the left and right arrows, move between Overview, Stages in opening order, and Topics without wrapping. Content links open the view that holds their target, including earlier revisions inside disclosures. Old fragment links still work. Shortcuts do nothing while typing in an input, textarea, select, or editable content, inside media controls, or with a modifier key held.

Diagrams keep their intrinsic label size rather than shrinking to fit a narrow screen. Swipe or scroll inside a wide diagram to read the rest. For keyboard panning, Tab to the diagram and use the arrow keys; those keys do not move the page's reading stop while the diagram has focus. Tab out to resume reading navigation. Captured Mermaid source and historical Pele SVGs follow the same rule in both themes.

A new question puts one status beside its title, then its **Decision brief**. **Now** says where the work stands. A re-ask says what changed or why the earlier attempt did not settle the question. The recommendation gives the agent's reason, or says why it has none. **After you answer** tells you what the answer unblocks and how to undo it. The question's description stays visible. Older questions keep their recorded description and choices without an invented brief. Answer in the picker, not on the page.

Compare the options with the deciding figure beside them. In a narrow pane, the figure sits below the options. Click the figure or use **Enlarge figure** to open it at full width. Click **Close figure** or press Escape to close it and return focus. Caption and description stay with the figure. Option previews stay with their options.

Open **Sources and context** below the options for Background, Topic, and Result links, Today facts and their sources, Term notes, figure sources, and additional figures. Result-entry links lead to the cited finding, including earlier revisions inside disclosures. Open **Earlier attempt** on a re-ask to see the exact recorded words apart from the agent's reading. Old `-choices` and `-evidence` fragments still open their targets, even inside a folded question.

## Reader comments

Each Result revision has a separate Reader comments area. Choose **Add Reader comment**, select the whole Result block or one of its entries, enter a note, and choose **Save comment**. Comments are plain text, up to 4096 characters. The list names the Stage, Result, revision, and anchor, with the saved time.

To comment on a passage, select text in the Result and choose **Comment on selected text**. For keyboard selection, choose **Select text to comment**, select a passage in the read-only **Result text for selection** field with Shift and the arrow keys, then Tab to **Comment on selected text**. The comment form and saved comment show the exact quote. **Edit comment** changes only the note, preserving its anchor and revision. **Remove comment**, followed by **Confirm removal**, removes the saved note. These controls work with Tab and Enter.

Comments are saved to the Flow and appear after a reload, in another browser, and after a Local Flow host restart. Edits and removals also synchronize across browsers. A comment remains attached to the exact Result version on which the reader opened its form, even if the Result changes before saving. This includes focused `revise.set` and `revise.add` edits within a whole-Result revision. Open **Earlier revisions** to read its original content and quote. Comments never move onto later text.

**Copy feedback**, below the Flow heading, copies every saved Reader comment across all Stages and revisions as one plain-text prompt. Each comment names its Stage, Result, revision, target, quoted passage, and note. Block and entry comments quote the Result title or entry label. Copying feedback does not send it to the agent.

The comments endpoint shares Quiz progress's Flow-scoped HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookie. Writes require that cookie, the host's own Origin, and `application/json`. Comments are stored separately from authored records. They never change authored content, answers, workflow progress, or the authored Flow revision, and never answer a question or count as consent.

When the user points at Reader comments, `show` lists them separately in `reader_comments`. Each record has `id`, `stage`, `result`, `revision`, `edit`, `anchor`, `text`, and `time`. Together, `revision` and `edit` identify the captured version: `edit` is the focused edit's Flow revision, or `null` before a focused edit. An anchor is `{ kind: "block" }`, `{ kind: "entry", entry: "<entry id>" }`, or `{ kind: "text", quote: "<selected passage>" }`. Older comments recover their version from recorded capture times when that identifies it unambiguously. Otherwise they have `version_unknown: true` and no `edit`; Copy feedback labels them as unknown without quoting current content. No notification or automatic context injection delivers comments to the agent.

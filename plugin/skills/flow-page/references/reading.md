# Read the Flow page

Read this reference when navigating the page or explaining its keyboard controls.

The Reading tab shows one main sequence across Stages in first-publication order. The Jump row follows that sequence. Each block names its Stage, state, and parent when known. Backgrounds, Topic notes, questions, Results, Assumed picks, and summaries keep their original positions. Answers and Result revisions update those blocks in place, and earlier Result revisions remain available in disclosures.

Historical content whose exact publication order was not recorded sits behind an explicit retained-order boundary. That section is not a claim of cross-type chronology. New contributions follow it in durable publication order.

`n` cycles through actionable questions, newest in-progress Stage first, then older in-progress Stages. Finished Stages, retired questions, settled answers, and superseded asks are excluded. Stage labels include ticket ids when known. Unticketed repeats get distinct labels.

`t` or the Stages button opens a clickable Stage-only tree with each Stage's state and open-question count. A continued Stage sits under its parent. This tree does not describe ticket dependencies. Stage links open their Overview context. The Topics tab indexes shared notes in the main reading sequence.

`h` and `l`, or the left and right arrows, select and focus tabs. `j` and `k`, or the down and up arrows, select reading stops, update the URL fragment, and move focus to the target. Content links reveal their destination, including earlier revisions inside disclosures. Shortcuts do nothing while typing in an input, textarea, select, or editable content.

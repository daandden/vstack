vstack Update report

Read each Upstream change beside its vstack proposal. Each row keeps its own baseline and decision. Confirmation happens through ask. Removed and renamed questions have no recommended option.

Judgment gates

Adapt and merge proposals use per-row three-way merges. Reason checks, every ADR, and the Fan-out review of changed items and their vstack callers run before confirmation. Unfixed review findings default to Hold. A held row never blocks the rest of its Upstream.

Principle selection

The re-run flags expected picks below 0.70 and non-allowed picks above 0.80. Scores from 0.70 through 0.80 are borderline.
